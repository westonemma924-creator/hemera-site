Reporting Readiness Check

Ten questions, about three minutes. You get a score, your weakest area and one thing to fix this week. Your answers stay in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Numbers: what I measure

I can name the 5 to 7 numbers that matter most for my business this week.

Each of those numbers has one clear source (a file, a system, a person).

Each number has one owner who is responsible for it being right.

2. Signals: when I react

For each number I know the size of change that would make me act.

I compare every week with the previous week in the same way (same period, same definition).

3. Action: what follows

My weekly report ends with decisions or actions, each with an owner and a date.

I know who reads the report and what they need to decide.

4. Time and safety: how I do it

I know how long my weekly report takes me, and it takes less than one hour.

The report is produced the same way every week (a template or a checklist).

I know what data I must not paste into an AI tool (names, client data, confidential numbers).
